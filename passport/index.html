Format
For a photo that passes
  • Have someone else take it with the rear camera, ~1.5 m away, phone at eye height.
  • Face a window or diffuse light. No shadows on face or background.
  • Plain background as required by the format. Neutral face, mouth closed.
  • Frame loosely — you crop, rotate and size it afterwards.

Everything runs on your phone. No photo leaves the device. Requirements change — always check the linked official source before submitting.
